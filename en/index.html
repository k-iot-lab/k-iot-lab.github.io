Platform · Architecture

More than a one-time alert.

A simple "water detected" signal can't tell you where the danger is, whether the water is still rising, or who needs to act. GITEUL keeps reading changes on site and connects judgment and response in one flow.

01 IoT Device (GSF100) Continuously measures water level and device status at low power.
02 Server Judges a 4-level alert against each site's thresholds and keeps the history.
03 AI Prediction Looks at the rate of change together with rainfall to predict when risk will be reached.
04 App · Monitoring Shares the same situation through maps and alerts, and carries the response forward.
